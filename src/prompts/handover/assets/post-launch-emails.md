# Post-Launch Email Sequence — framework (deliverable 04)

Purpose: after Episode 2 drops and the doors open, convert the warmed list into paid members before the founding window closes. ~10 emails over ~2–3 weeks. This is the sequence that scaled the gold-example launches (per the framework doc, post-marketing emails took one launch from $20k→$34k MRR).

## Sequence arc (default 10 emails)

| # | Timing | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 (doors open) | "The doors are open." Information vs immersion; this is a community, not another shelf course. First CTA to join. |
| 2 | Day +1 | Transformation story #1 — a single vivid member/creator story tied to `transformation`. `[[PROOF/STORY]]`. |
| 3 | Day +2 | Social proof / momentum — "people can't stop talking inside," early wins. `[[TESTIMONIAL]]` x2. |
| 4 | Day +4 | Reframe — "more than just another <category> course"; why environment beats information. |
| 5 | Day +6 | Transformation story #2 — different life area than #2. |
| 6 | Day +8 | Objection-crush — "let's be honest about the excuses" ("I'll find it free," "no time," "what if it doesn't work"). |
| 7 | Day +10 | Real transformations montage — 3 short member arcs; introduce the price/founding deadline. |
| 8 | Day +12 | Price/urgency — "<founder rate> today, more tomorrow." `[[PRICE INCREASE / FOUNDER WINDOW DATE]]`. |
| 9 | Day +13 | Cost reframe — "<price> vs the life you've been putting off" (compare to coffees/dinners). |
| 10 | Day +14 | "My last message" — final call, honest challenge, founding window closes. |

Scale down to 7 by cutting one story + one urgency email; never cut #1, #6, #8, #10.

## Rules
- First person, from `creator_first`; sign each.
- Every email = ONE CTA button line: `[Join the community]` → `[[SKOOL JOIN LINK]]`.
- Price stated is the **founding/entry** price from `tiers[]` (e.g. "$27/mo"). Urgency = founding window closing; only claim a literal price increase if confirmed → else `[[PRICE INCREASE DATE]]`.
- Stories and testimonials are ALWAYS placeholders — never fabricate member results, names, or numbers (guardrails §2, CDF-3).
- Vary structure: some emails are a single story, some are teaching, some are direct pitch. Don't make all 10 the same shape.
- Domain caution applies (no income/health guarantees).
