# EXAMPLE B — Fitness, upgrade-focused (real MeisterBody set)
> Bold + warm fitness voice. Two techniques worth stealing: (1) every upgrade DM offers TWO CTAs — `[YES UPGRADE NOW]` (plans link) AND `[BOOK ONBOARDING CALL]` (a Calendly link) — the call catches fence-sitters; (2) the upgrade angle is IDENTITY + accountability ("the version of you that's waiting"), not features. Note: this hand-made set used Premium/VIP naming and different annual prices than the DNA — a reminder the machine follows the CURRENT DNA, but the voice here is the benchmark.

**FREE → PREMIUM UPGRADE (6 DMs) — representative beats:**
- *DM1 (identity):* "Somewhere out there is a version of you that's stronger, healthier, who's unlocked their full potential. That version isn't a fantasy — it's built one micro-decision at a time. Right now you've got the Fundamentals classroom — the knowledge. But knowledge alone never got anyone off the couch. Accountability does. That's what Premium unlocks."
- *DM2 (why results stall):* "You already know what to do… so why aren't you there yet? Knowledge without accountability is just trivia. What moves the needle is someone in your corner who catches you before you quit."
- *DM3 (the system, real perks):* "Premium ($27/mo or $227/yr): personal accountability coach (1:1) · advanced protocols + habit trackers · weekly video-submission feedback · monthly Q&A calls with me · recorded coaching library. The coach alone is worth it."
- *DM4 (skip a level → VIP):* "Some of you are 'cannonball into the deep end' people. VIP ($57/mo or $477/yr): Mastery classroom · monthly VIP Mastermind calls · in-person meets & retreats · priority support · a free month of 1:1 with me (annual)."
- *DM5 (proof):* real member testimonials → then "they didn't have superhuman willpower. They had accountability. That could be you in 90 days." *(In generated output, testimonials are `[[TESTIMONIAL]]` — never fabricated.)*
- *DM6 (the decision):* "$27/month. Less than your coffee habit. You're already here. You've already started. Time to go all in."
CTAs each DM: `👉 [YES UPGRADE NOW]` (plans) + `📞 [BOOK ONBOARDING CALL]` (Calendly). Merge tag `#NAME#`.

**DECLINED (3) / CANCELLED (3) — key beats:** declined = soft heads-up → "it's not just content you lose, it's the people (your coach, the calls, the community in your corner)" → final retry notice. Cancelled = "door stays open, no hard feelings — mind sharing why?" → "most people don't fall off because they don't know what to do; they fall off because accountability stops" → "your spot is here whenever you're ready." Asking for the cancel reason (feedback loop) is a nice touch.
