There's a story inside you.

You know the one. It's been living in your heart for years — something real, something hard-won, something that could matter to another woman who needs to hear it.

But it stays silent. You write a paragraph and stop. You wonder if you're even a "real writer." You worry it won't come out the way it deserves to. Or maybe you're afraid — of being judged, of being too much, of being seen.

So the story waits. And the world waits with it.

I'm Laura Cornell. For 30 years I've walked alongside women through their deepest transformations. I've written four bestselling books. And in all that work, I've learned one thing above everything else:

Your story doesn't just want to be written. It needs to be witnessed — it is longing to be shared.

That's why I created the Story Medicine Writers Community.

It's not a course, although there are courses inside. It's primarily a circle. A place where brave women bring their truth — messy, unfinished, raw, sacred — and are held by other women who understand why it matters.

Because the real transformation doesn't happen on a blank page alone. It happens when you share something vulnerable and a woman says, "Oh my Goddess, I felt that too." It happens when someone witnesses your words and says, "You touched me. Your story matters."

That is the medicine.

There are three ways to join us.

The Reading Room is completely free. This is your way in. Read powerful stories from women just like you. Join live book launches and meet the authors. Try your first Story Seeds — short, embodied writing prompts. No pressure. No commitment. Just come and feel what this community is.

The Writers' Circle is $44 a month. This is where your writing practice gets held and witnessed. Weekly writing prompts, two live Story Medicine Circles with me every month, Writing Together sessions, and access to evergreen mini-courses. You stop writing alone — and everything changes.

And right now, to celebrate our Grand Opening, the first women who subscribe annually will receive a private Constellation session with me — a $195 value, yours free. When they're gone, they're gone.

The Author Level is $197 a month and includes everything in The Writers' Circle, plus monthly group coaching directly with me, Open Writing Retreat Days, and access to advanced story and sacred business courses. This is for the woman who's ready to go all in on her voice.

And wherever you start, this is the beginning of a longer journey. You don't need to know which path is yours yet. You just need to begin.

The world needs your story. Your experience, your wisdom, your truth — there is a woman out there right now who needs to read exactly what only you can write.

Come join us. The Reading Room is free and it's waiting for you. Or step straight into The Writers' Circle and let the community hold you.

Either way — begin. We look forward to supporting you.

## CANCELLATION VIDEO SCRIPT (2–3 min · first person)

Hi there,

I see you're thinking about canceling your membership. Before you go, I just want to say... I get it. Life gets busy. Money gets tight. Sometimes we have to make hard choices. And that's okay. No matter what, you're welcome here.

But I also want to be real with you for a moment. Because sometimes we cancel things right when we're about to experience the breakthrough.

Think about what's been shifting for you. Have you started to feel less alone? Have you shared something vulnerable and been witnessed? Are the Circles starting to feel like a safe place to be yourself?

Sometimes we run at the moment things are just beginning to change. Don't let that be now.

If it's a money thing — I hear you. Let me offer you something.

You can downgrade to The Reading Room. It's completely free. You stay connected, you can still explore the author interviews and try the Story Seeds. Many of my best women started here and grew into The Writers' Circle when they were ready. No pressure. No cost. Come back anytime.

Or you can downgrade to The Writers' Circle at $44 a month. Stay in the circle with your 2 monthly Circles, the Writing Together sessions, and the weekly prompts. That's less than a coffee a day.

Or take a pause. You can pause your membership for 30 days. No charge. No judgment. Come back when you're ready.

Or if you truly need to cancel, I respect that. But know the door is always open. You can rejoin anytime.

Because Story Medicine isn't really about the courses. It's about belonging to a circle of women who understand. That's the medicine — and that's something you can't get alone.

So before you go, ask yourself: what if you stayed for just one more month? What if you showed up to the next Circle?

Your voice matters. And the world needs to hear it. The door is always open. Come back anytime.
