## THE DOCUSERIES EVENT LAUNCH
Style reference: Iman Gadzhi — cinematic, documentary-style, direct-to-camera. Two episodes. Not a webinar, not an ad — an emotional arc.

- **Episode 1 = the problem.** Make the viewer feel deeply understood, then feel the full cost of staying where they are. End by breadcrumbing the solution so they come back.
- **Episode 2 = the solution — and the solution IS the community (Skool).** Briefly re-affirm the problem, then spend the bulk (≥20 min) pitching Skool: what's inside, why it's the answer, the proof, the CTA. By the time the offer is on the table, the viewer has already decided.
- **Launch mechanic:** first 72 hours = FOUNDING MEMBER window at the founding rate, grandfathered permanently; price rises after. The urgency engine.
- **Tone:** the guide who lived the story and has seen the pattern a hundred times. Authority from recognition, never hype. Ep1 slow and heavy; Ep2 builds and lifts into the offer.

### Episode 1 arc
Cold Open (hit a nerve, no intro, title card after) → Authority (lived it, pattern-recognition) → Identify the Problem (name pains one at a time; end on "the real problem is the model") → Deepen the Problem (heaviest section; hidden costs; 12-month trajectory) → Breadcrumb the Solution (answer comes in Ep2).

### Episode 2 arc
Re-affirm the Problem (<2 min) → Reveal the Solution (paradigm shift, feels like a revelation) → The Results (paint the after; anchor with real proof) → Pitch the Community (bulk of runtime; what's inside, who it's for, stack value) → The Offer + Founding Rate (pricing once, lead annual, 72h mechanic) → CTA + Close (one next step: join).

### Proof it works (from the playbook)
- One event generated $20k MRR / $240k ARR, scaled to $34k MRR with the post-launch emails.
- Another collected $67k cash in week one and $20k MRR in month one.

## VARIANT B — Multi-guest panel series (HCC "The 2026 Divide")
> Format variant: a multi-day event where the HOST carries Episode 1 (the foundation + the problem) and GUEST interviews carry the middle days, each guest sharing ONE practice, with the pitch landing at the end. For communities with `guest_sessions: Yes` and a roster of aligned experts. Below: the Episode 1 host open, word-for-word excerpt — same Problem arc, different delivery.

**Episode 1 — host open (verbatim excerpt):**
"Today is Day 1 of something we've been working on for a long time. You're watching Episode One of The 2026 Divide: Those Who Know vs. Those Who Embody — the beginning of a week-long event featuring voices you already know and trust. Here's how this unfolds: Today, we lay the foundation. Tomorrow, [guest] joins us. Then from [dates], five one-on-one interviews with some of the biggest guests we've ever had. Set a reminder and turn on notifications so you don't miss a day — every episode drops at [time]."

Then the **problem arc** (note the villain is the model, not the viewer):
"Somewhere along the way, spiritual growth turned into spiritual entertainment. Watching instead of practicing. Listening instead of applying. Collecting ideas instead of living them. Short-form video amplified it — deep ideas reduced to 30-second clips that feel exciting but change nothing. People sound awake. They can quote the teachings. But they don't feel safe in their bodies. That's what happens when entertainment replaces embodiment."

Close: re-state the schedule, the daily drop time, and the CTA — subscribe + show up daily. (Guests promote their own day to their own lists — see `example-pre-launch-guest-emails.md`.)

## VARIANT C — 2-day LIVE event hosted inside Skool (Sianna → ALCHEMY: Soul Sanctuary)
> Format variant: the arc mapped onto a live event tied to a real calendar moment (a solstice). Day 1 = the problem (live), Day 2 = the solution + pitch (live). The community IS the offer; the pitch is a repeatable "Sell the Community + FAQ + go-annual-hard" block run at the end of each session.
- **Spine:** one truth — *"we were never meant to awaken alone."* Problem = the loneliness of the modern path (practicing in isolation, no circle to witness you). Solution = belonging; the community as the medicine.
- **Day 1 (Opening → maps to Ep1 Problem):** cold-open into the longing they live with → establish authority → name the problem plainly (awakening alone) → deepen the cost of going it alone → breadcrumb the Sanctuary, revealed tomorrow.
- **Day 2 (Teaching + re-invite → maps to Ep2 Solution):** re-affirm the ache → reveal the Sanctuary as the answer → walk the tiers → lead the annual founding rate → land the offer.
- **Tiers pitched:** Premium $44/mo or $333/yr · VIP $97/mo or $777/yr.
- **Founding window:** ~6 weeks (through a fixed date), tied to a real challenge kickoff (a 40-day journey starting on the full moon) — the long end of the 72h–1-month range. The urgency is a real event, not an arbitrary countdown.
- **The lever:** the biggest bonuses (the 40-day challenge, a livestreamed celebration) are **annual-only** — that's what makes "go annual" land.
